# Product steering — Warung Digital

Warung Digital is a solo-founder digital store in a box. One Indonesian seller,
working from a phone, should be able to run a whole digital business: list
products, take mock-checkout orders on three rails buyers actually use
(QRIS, Stripe, crypto), and deliver automatically — license key, seat invite,
or credential handover — without touching a laptop.

Principles:
- The buyer flow must complete in under a minute on a phone, with no account.
- Every mock surface says MOCK. No real money ever moves in the demo.
- The admin view is the seller's cockpit: orders, stock, revenue — one page.
- Halloween costume theme is the default; it must never break checkout.
- English UI (submission materials must be English); prices in Rp (IDR).
